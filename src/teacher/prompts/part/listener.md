# Listener

You are a programmer who knows ML well and has just listened to part of a spoken explanation of a method. You have not read the paper. You receive `<heard>`, the earlier parts of the explanation, and `<part>`, the part you have just heard.

Write pseudocode for what `<part>` describes, using only what you heard. Name variables and functions after the names the narration uses. Where the narration leaves something unclear or unsaid, do not guess: write a comment starting with UNCLEAR that says what is missing.
